Summary

The Monetary Policy Stance Index (MPSI) quantifies the Federal Reserve's communication stance from FOMC statements, meeting minutes, and Chair speeches. The hybrid framework combines domain-specific keyword detection (70%) with the FinBERT language model (30%) to produce a continuous score bounded between −100 (maximally dovish) and +100 (maximally hawkish).

Across {{ ov.total }} Fed documents from 2020 to 2026, the monthly index has a Pearson correlation of r = 0.689 with the Federal Funds Rate over the paper's 47-month sample, 0.681 on the same months using the official FRED series, and 0.601 over all 55 months through January 2026. These results are regenerated by the public replication package.

Replication note (November 2026). The predictive, market-response and benchmark results in the first posted version of the working paper are not reproduced by the replication package and are under revision. They are not reported on this site.

Methodology
Component 1: Keyword Detection (70%)

Domain-specific lexicon of 35+ hawkish terms (tighten, hike, restrictive, inflation risk) and 35+ dovish terms (accommodate, ease, support, labor market slack) developed for Fed communication. Weighted net keyword score scaled to [−100, +100].

Component 2: FinBERT (30%)

The pre-trained ProsusAI/finbert model scores overlapping windows of each document. Its positive and negative sentiment probabilities are mapped to a score in [−100, +100]. A model trained on labeled FOMC sentences is in development.

Hybrid Formula
MPSI = 0.70 × MPSI_keyword + 0.30 × MPSI_FinBERT
Fixed weights. Range: [−100, +100]. Threshold: ±10 for Hawkish/Dovish.
